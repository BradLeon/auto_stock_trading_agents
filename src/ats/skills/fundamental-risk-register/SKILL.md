# Company Risk Register

List the company-specific risks an analyst should keep watching for ONE company, each with an observable
falsifier. These complement the earnings thesis all year.

- Company-specific only: key person, governance, customer concentration, a pending legal or regulatory case,
  dependence on a single supplier, a contested product transition. Layer-wide risks (an export rule on the whole
  industry, a commodity price cycle) do not belong here.
- `falsifier`: the observable development that would show the risk has materialised (e.g. "CEO 离任或被董事会调查",
  "最大客户收入占比跌破 30%"). Make it checkable from news or filings.
- Keep a previous item unless the given material shows it no longer applies; keep its key stable.
- At most eight items. Chinese labels and text. No advice, no positions.
